# Code of Conduct

We are building a welcoming learning community. Be respectful, patient, constructive, and inclusive.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different abilities, identities, cultures, and levels of experience.
- Give useful feedback about the work, never attacks against the person.
- Protect privacy and credit the ideas and work of others.
- Accept corrections and help beginners participate safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, humiliation, plagiarism, impersonation, sharing another person's private information, account sharing, manipulated contribution metrics, or dangerous challenges are not allowed.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
