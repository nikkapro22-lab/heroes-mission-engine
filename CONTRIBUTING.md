# Contributing to HEROES Mission Engine

Thank you for helping create ethical missions for real-world growth. Beginners are welcome.

## Before you begin

1. Create your own GitHub account.
2. Read the README, mission format, ethical guidelines, and accessibility guide.
3. Ask the facilitator for one unclaimed mission number.
4. Fork this repository into your own account.
5. Change only the file assigned to you under `packs/es/`.

## Your contribution

Write from your own knowledge and experience. AI may help organize, edit, or check your work, but you must review every line and remain responsible for it. Never invent experiences, results, evidence, or sources.

Every mission pack must include: purpose, three progressive missions, optional evidence, reflection, an accessible alternative, safety boundaries, and attribution.

## Submitting your work

1. Review the complete change with your AI assistant.
2. Confirm no personal data, secrets, private platform code, or copied material is present.
3. Commit the change only after you approve it.
4. Open one focused pull request against `main` using the repository template.
5. Do not merge the pull request. A maintainer will review it.

By contributing, you agree that your work is original and may be distributed under the MIT License.
