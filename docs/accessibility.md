# Accessibility Guide

Each mission pack must include an equivalent alternative, not merely an easier afterthought.

Consider participants with limited time, energy, mobility, vision, hearing, attention, literacy, internet access, money, privacy, or emotional capacity. Use plain language, short steps, headings, and formats that do not depend only on color, sound, images, or continuous connectivity.

An accessible alternative should preserve the purpose of the mission while changing its method, duration, environment, or evidence. Avoid requiring purchases, public disclosure, travel, live video, or physical exertion unless a free and safe equivalent is provided.
