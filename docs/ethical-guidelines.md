# Ethical Guidelines

Every mission must protect the participant's agency, dignity, privacy, and safety.

- Participation and evidence are voluntary.
- Progress is based on real action, not vanity metrics.
- Never use humiliation, punishment, artificial scarcity, coercion, or loss of earned progress.
- Do not encourage harmful streaks, risky exposure, deception, or unnecessary spending.
- Health and emotional missions are educational and must not diagnose or replace professional care.
- Legal and financial missions must remain general and educational.
- Ask only for proportional evidence and offer a private alternative.
- Make recovery, rest, and restarting possible without shame.
- Disclose meaningful AI assistance and keep a human responsible for the final work.
