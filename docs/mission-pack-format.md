# Mission Pack Format

Use one Markdown file named `NN-short-topic.md` under `packs/es/`.

## Required sections

1. **Title and summary** — who the pack is for and the real-world result.
2. **Purpose** — why this ability matters.
3. **Initiation mission** — a small action that can begin today.
4. **Practice mission** — a repeatable or slightly harder action.
5. **Ascent mission** — an integration or community action.
6. **Optional evidence** — a privacy-respecting way to verify progress. A private reflection is always valid.
7. **Reflection** — two or three questions about learning and next steps.
8. **Accessible alternative** — a version for different energy, mobility, time, money, connectivity, literacy, or privacy needs.
9. **Safety boundary** — risks, stop conditions, and when professional help is appropriate.
10. **Attribution** — contributor name or GitHub username and any openly licensed sources.

Write clear Spanish, use short steps, avoid promises of guaranteed outcomes, and keep the pack useful outside the HEROES platform.
